// how it works

From MRI Upload to
Annotated Result in 4 Steps

The full classification pipeline — from raw scan to explainability overlay — runs in under two seconds.

1
Upload Your MRI Scan
Drag and drop or browse for any JPG, PNG, BMP, or TIFF brain MRI image. The upload interface validates your file instantly and shows a live preview thumbnail before submission.
2
AI Processes the Scan
The image is resized to 224×224, RGB-converted, and normalized using EfficientNet preprocessing. The model runs inference through the frozen backbone and custom classification head in milliseconds.
3
Receive Your Classification
The top predicted class is displayed with a confidence percentage, and all four per-class softmax probabilities are shown as animated bars — giving you the full distribution, not just the winner.
4
Inspect the EigenCAM Heatmap
A real-time EigenCAM activation map highlights the MRI regions that most influenced the prediction — providing the clinical transparency necessary for radiologist review and decision-making.

Deep Learning Pipeline
TensorFlow/Keras powers both models — EfficientNetB0 with ImageNet transfer learning for classification and a custom U-Net trained on BraTS 2020 for binary volumetric segmentation with hybrid BCE+Dice loss.
🔭
Explainability Engine
EigenCAM is computed in-memory using NumPy SVD on the final convolutional feature maps. No gradient computation required — stable, fast, and returned as a base64 PNG in the same API response as the prediction.
🚀
Zero-Setup Deployment
The entire platform runs on Hugging Face Spaces — no GPU required. EfficientNetB0's 5.3M parameters fit comfortably in CPU inference, achieving <2s prediction latency including EigenCAM overlay generation.
